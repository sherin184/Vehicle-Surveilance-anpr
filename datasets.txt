## Datasets

The project uses publicly available datasets for vehicle and license plate
detection/recognition experiments.

- [Indian Vehicle License Plate Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/dsv/3369458)
- [Indian License Plates with Labels – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kedarsai/indian-license-plates-with-labels)
- [Indian Number Plates Dataset – DataCluster](https://github.com/datacluster-labs/Indian-Number-Plates-Dataset)
- [Indian License Plate Detection – Roboflow](https://universe.roboflow.com/atria-university/indian-license-plate-detection-6tmbr-ixo3p)
- [UFPR-ALPR Dataset](https://web.inf.ufpr.br/vri/databases/ufpr-alpr/)
- [CCPD License Plate Dataset](https://github.com/detectrecog/ccpd)

> Datasets are not included in this repository. Please download them
> directly from their respective sources and follow their licensing terms.
